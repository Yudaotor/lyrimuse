v1.9.0

Latest28 September 202615 new · 13 improved · 11 fixed

New

  • Added Soda Music, KKBOX and Amazon Music as built-in players, bringing the total to eight
  • Lyrics for the next songs in your queue are now ready before they play
  • Lyrics your players already saved on your Mac are now used directly, without a search
  • The lyrics window now shows background vocals, overlapping duet lines and Apple Music-style word emphasis
  • Added a mini size to the lyrics window, plus background, colour and font options
  • Refreshed the desktop lyrics with a theme gallery, interlude dots and drag-to-resize
  • Long lines can now be split, and short ones joined, to fit the desktop lyrics, Dynamic Island and menu bar
  • Added album notes and artist bios for the song that is playing
  • The Dynamic Island gains shuffle and repeat for Apple Music and steps aside for full-screen apps
  • Added optional ad skipping and better lyrics timing for YouTube Music videos
  • Added new Last.fm stats: listening hours, artist regions, rank changes and loved tracks
  • Scrobbles can now match Last.fm's own spelling of artists and song titles
  • Added monthly and yearly listening summaries, and Telegram as a push channel
  • You can now add any app as a player without waiting for it to play
  • The lyrics manager can now fill in missing lyrics and tells you when it finishes

Improved

  • From this version on, updates keep Accessibility, Automation and Full Disk Access permissions
  • Your lyrics library is now better protected against damaged files and failed saves
  • Lyrics appear noticeably faster, and more songs get word timing, translations and readings
  • Improved lyrics matching, so fewer songs get the wrong version or misaligned timing
  • Covers now load sharper from most sources, and faster in lists
  • Lyrimuse now uses much less CPU, memory and disk space
  • Lyrimuse copes better with rate limits, outages and changes on the services it relies on
  • Renamed the background service to the lyrics engine, and settings now apply without restarting it
  • Streamlined the first-run guide and made permissions clearer throughout Settings
  • Last.fm stats are more accurate and sync faster, with missing covers filled in from your library
  • Failed Last.fm scrobbles are now resent, and you are notified if the connection drops
  • Export Diagnostics now includes the complete logs, with credentials removed
  • Polished the wording and layout across Settings and the lyrics manager

Fixed

  • Fixed lyrics and controls breaking when a browser video took over Now Playing
  • Fixed lyrics freezing while AppleScript or Spotify stopped responding for a moment
  • Fixed Apple Music links, covers and progress failing in some regions and languages
  • Fixed several cases where songs found no lyrics or showed the wrong artist
  • Fixed Last.fm issues with music video scrobbles, play counts and switching accounts
  • Fixed edits in the lyrics manager landing on the wrong song or going stale
  • Fixed lyrics export, timing offsets and translations falling out of sync in edge cases
  • Fixed web players showing songs late or with the browser's icon
  • Fixed cover flicker, stray clicks and display glitches in the desktop lyrics, lyrics window, Dynamic Island and menu bar
  • Fixed blurry or jagged lyrics on non-Retina displays and a menu bar that kept resizing
  • Fixed custom fonts, light themes, shortcuts, update checks and Apple Music reconnect in Settings

v1.8.0

18 September 202615 new · 6 improved · 17 fixed

New

  • Added Apple Music as an eleventh lyrics source — the only one carrying Apple's own word-by-word timing, and the only one that asks you to connect the account in Settings, once every six months
  • Added a full rescan that re-picks every lyric in the library under today's matching rules
  • A full rescan skips hand-corrected lyrics, confirmed instrumentals and tracks whose timeline you tuned
  • You can now import your own .ttf and .otf fonts for the lyrics
  • Split the karaoke fill into two independent colours, one sung and one unsung
  • Added a thickness setting for the frosted background behind the desktop lyrics
  • Added a switch for the playback buttons that appear when you hover the desktop lyrics
  • You can now pick a font family for the menu bar lyrics
  • Added the second line and font size to the menu bar quick settings panel
  • The menu bar panel now offers one button that starts the music again when nothing is playing
  • Added back and forward keys to the Settings window
  • Replaced the system colour panel with a smaller picker of our own
  • The menu bar preview now draws the max-width limit
  • AMLL now looks a track up by the ID the system provides, instead of relying on NetEase and QQ being switched on
  • New installs now scrobble collaborations with smart credit; existing installs keep their setting

Improved

  • Moved the score breakdown into the expanded row in the lyrics manager
  • Opening the lyrics manager is faster on a large library
  • Reworked the selected state of the player cards: the card lifts off the page, and the accent colour stays on the border and the checkmark
  • Lined up both toolbar rows in the editor stages, column by column
  • The background service no longer does work nothing asked for: no web accent colour without a relay, no ListenBrainz without a token
  • Removed the "N/9" badge and the "thin evidence only" filter; the full count is on the track's decision sheet

Fixed

  • Fixed Apple Music lyrics stopping when another app took over the system's now-playing slot
  • Fixed the menu bar lyrics flickering, disappearing, and rebuilding in the middle of a line
  • Fixed three ways per-word romanisation came out wrong, including a blank word eating a syllable
  • Fixed Chinese being read as Japanese in lines that splice kana into a Chinese clause
  • Fixed DJ mix segments borrowing the studio version's lyrics
  • The search sheet now says why a track has no usable lyrics
  • Fixed a music video's length being taken as the song's length
  • Fixed catalog matching ignoring the artist, crossing storefronts, and settling for the first album track within tolerance
  • Fixed parenthesised name lists and the 组曲 prefix derailing the search
  • Fixed the Dynamic Island's black bar not matching the real notch height
  • Fixed the equalizer's position when it has an ear to itself and when the island is expanded
  • Fixed the desktop lyrics control bar opening from outside the lyrics text
  • Fixed a Dock icon staying around for minimized and auxiliary windows
  • Fixed follow-the-cover blanking out the colour theme row
  • Fixed the Apple Music automation card not showing on the default player setting
  • Fixed a motion cover that could not be checked being reported as a mismatch
  • Fixed player chips not starting from the left on every row, a stray rule in front of sub-rows, and the decision sheet's inputs not lining up

v1.7.0

13 September 202617 new · 6 improved · 22 fixed

New

  • Deezer joins as a tenth lyrics source, and it covers a lot of ground the other nine were thin on: songs in French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese and Korean that used to come back with a poor match, or nothing at all, now have line-by-line lyrics
  • Album art moves in the lyrics window when Apple Music has a motion cover for the record; there's a switch to turn it off
  • Apple Music radio works properly: lyrics keep up with each track instead of drifting, and while the host is talking you see the station's name and logo rather than the last song
  • Radio lyrics have their own timing offset, so a station you listen to often can be nudged once and stay right
  • Settings can be searched: the matching row is highlighted and scrolled to, and a row tucked inside a collapsed group opens up on its own
  • Updates are installed from a Software Update page inside Settings, with the release notes and the progress in the same window
  • The desktop lyrics can be pinned to the top center or the bottom center above the Dock, or keep floating wherever you drag them
  • Font, weight and size for the Dynamic Island's lyric text
  • During an ad the island goes black and tells you how long is left and which ad of how many this is; YouTube Music web ads get a skip button
  • Duet lines stay near the center, so widening the overlay gives long lines more room instead of pushing the two voices apart
  • Last.fm can skip individual players, so plays from a player you'd rather not log stay out of your history; ListenBrainz is untouched
  • When a player reports no album name, one is looked up and included in the scrobble, so those plays no longer land without an album
  • "Show in Spotify" jumps to the track that's playing, and the shuffle button is hidden when Spotify says shuffling isn't allowed
  • Lyrics Manager explains a pick in plain terms: what the runner-up was short on, which sources returned the same words, and what was actually searched for
  • Lyrics Manager marks how many sources answered when a lyric was picked, and you can filter for the thin ones
  • Buttons on the desktop lyrics and the island respond to hover and press, and the island's quick actions show what they do
  • A newly recognized player is announced on the Dynamic Island as well

Improved

  • The Settings sidebar was rebuilt to sit closer to System Settings
  • Every Settings page opens with its own title and a line saying what it is for, and the window is taller so the desktop-lyrics section fits without scrolling
  • The "Web" row in the lyrics window's info panel links to the current player's own page for the track instead of listing every platform
  • The lyrics editor shows the words only; the tag lines no longer push them to a second screen
  • Tracks without lyrics are sorted more carefully: ones where no source answered at all are now told apart from ones that really have no lyrics, so a bad moment on the network stops looking like a verdict
  • The onboarding ends with a little confetti

Fixed

  • A Japanese original is no longer displaced by an "(English ver.)" release
  • A Japanese song with a romanized title no longer comes back with a single candidate
  • A space between a Japanese artist's family and given name no longer leaves every source empty-handed
  • A track no longer gets lyrics credited to a completely unrelated artist
  • "(Live)" and its Chinese equivalent count as the same version, so the right recording from that concert is no longer marked down
  • Tracks uploaded by re-post channels, with the artist written into the title, can be found now
  • The source that answered last had its words thrown away, so a song could end up with a worse match, or none at all, even though a source had found it
  • A track that came up empty once no longer sits on "Searching for lyrics…" forever
  • A brief network problem is no longer recorded as "this track has no lyrics"
  • A failed lookup of an artist's other spellings is no longer taken for "this artist has none", which used to keep every later song by that artist from being searched under the name the sources actually use
  • Stray character codes that some sources leave in the lyric text (they&apos;re) are cleaned up, existing lyrics included
  • A real song on YouTube Music is no longer labelled "Ad" for its whole duration
  • After an ad on YouTube Music, the next song no longer starts with a few blank seconds where nothing is shown
  • Spotify's progress no longer runs slow until you pause and resume
  • "Follow algorithm upgrades" no longer quietly stops working after a few upgrades — which also means a throttled background pass may move some tracks to a better match over the hours after you update
  • A track you're hearing for the first time no longer shows a listen count of 2
  • A Last.fm backfill that succeeded is no longer reported as failed, the recent plays refresh right after it, and it always says what it did
  • The row of playback buttons under the desktop lyrics appears only when the pointer is over the words, not anywhere inside the window
  • The dark specks on the island's small cover art are gone
  • Word-level romanization stays under the right characters on every line, not only the one being sung
  • Testing one lyrics source no longer keeps spinning after its result is already on screen
  • Clicking the Dock icon reliably brings the window back

v1.6.0

7 September 202614 new · 11 improved · 6 fixed

Spotify lyric sync got a precision overhaul — the constant slight drift after resuming, the backwards jump when pausing, and the lag after ads are all gone. The menu bar can now show two lyric lines (next line, translation or romanization under the current one — the Dynamic Island has the same option), Migu Music joins as the ninth lyrics source, you can choose when a play scrobbles to Last.fm, and the settings pages went through a full reorganization with trimmed copy. Worth knowing before you upgrade: the matching algorithm is stricter about Cantonese-vs-Mandarin versions, so a throttled background pass may switch a few tracks to a better match over the following hours (the "follow algorithm upgrades" toggle freezes picks if you prefer); appearance settings you never touched now use recalibrated defaults, so a few surfaces may look slightly different; and the old global karaoke switch became three per-surface toggles (your previous choice carries over).

New

  • The menu bar can show a second lyric row — next line, translation or romanization under the current line; the Dynamic Island capsule has the same option
  • Migu Music joins as a lyrics source — nine in total now
  • Choose when a play counts for Last.fm: the official 50% rule, 75%, 90%, or only when the track plays to the end; ListenBrainz is unaffected
  • Player linkage is per-player now: pick which players launch with Lyrimuse, which ones launch Lyrimuse, and optionally quit Lyrimuse once the players it follows have all quit
  • An opt-in beta channel: turn on "Receive beta updates" in Settings →About to try pre-release builds; turn it off to stay on stable
  • The notch's expanded header gains a quick-actions row: search lyrics, toggle lyrics, open settings, or close the island — one hover away
  • The notch's expanded state can be wider than the resting capsule —set both widths with one dual-slider; lyrics alignment gains an automatic option that follows duet parts
  • When nothing is playing, the notch shows the app icon and blends into the real notch; hovering opens a compact idle panel with resume / open-player / settings shortcuts — and the desktop overlay shows a "♪ Lyrimuse" mark instead of nothing
  • Lyrics Manager: retry every track still missing lyrics in one click (all of them, or just the filtered ones), and mark a track as instrumental yourself; tracks without lyrics are now classified in four tiers instead of a blanket red "no lyrics"
  • A storage-style lyrics library panel in Settings: total count, a proportion bar of word-synced / line-synced / plain / instrumental, translation and romanization tallies
  • The manual lyrics search window is resizable and draggable, each candidate shows title / artist / album on separate lines, candidates with identical text are labeled, and sources that couldn't be reached are reported separately (DNS / connection / server) instead of counting as "no result"
  • Picking lyrics from the overlay's quick-search keeps the window open, so you can switch sources and listen until one sounds right
  • Source priority is reordered by dragging now (arrows stay for keyboard and VoiceOver)
  • Japanese lyrics typed with simplified-Chinese glyphs by some sources are repaired back to proper kanji — rule-based, no lookup table

Improved

  • Spotify position accuracy: anchors are pinned at stream-event arrival (±20ms), stale anchor republishes are recognized and ignored, pauses freeze at the pause instant, and a one-shot probe corrects the late anchor after ads
  • Lyrics matching v15: Cantonese and Mandarin versions of the same song are inferred and judged across the whole candidate batch, so a correctly tagged "(粤语)" candidate no longer loses to the wrong-language one
  • Matching also got stricter along the way: bare "(Edit)" cuts no longer masquerade as the original, translations baked into the lyric body are split out, and candidates are ranked by how well their reported duration fits the track
  • Lyric sources you disabled are no longer queried at all
  • All lyric sources share a system-DNS-first, DoH-fallback connection path — fixes six sources being unreachable under VPN-pushed DNS
  • The alias retry round re-queries only the sources that are missing usable candidates, instead of all-or-nothing
  • Default source order re-ranked by measured adoption; trailing credit lines no longer inflate a candidate's duration score, and 指导/总监/策划/导演 credit lines are filtered too
  • Karaoke word-fill is a per-surface toggle now (overlay / island / menu bar); the Lyrics Window always renders word-by-word
  • Launch at login is a standard system login item now (visible in System Settings), and restarts run the app at proper UI priority —animations no longer risk being throttled
  • Settings copy was trimmed across the board — shorter titles, fewer lecturing subtitles, tighter help bubbles
  • A corrupted config file is never silently overwritten anymore: it's quarantined with a banner and an explicit recovery path, and background-service restarts now report their result in the settings window

Fixed

  • The notch no longer expands when the pointer is merely below it
  • Notch animations: collapsing no longer undershoots and crawls back, expanded content crossfades in place instead of sliding, and the equalizer bars no longer flicker during size changes
  • The menu bar preview in Settings no longer flashes when the status item is rebuilt
  • Play counts Last.fm was slow to index are re-probed with backoff instead of being stuck at "no count"; tapping a count shows exactly which spellings were merged into it
  • "Back to current track" in Lyrics Manager scrolls to the right row on the first click
  • The search sheet's source counter is derived from the source list itself — no more "0/8" next to "nine sources"

v1.5.0

3 September 202626 new · 17 fixed

The lyrics matching algorithm was overhauled — concert recordings should stop getting studio lyrics, and vice versa. YouTube Music and Spotify playing in a browser are now properly supported as players, and the UI speaks Traditional Chinese now. Worth knowing before you upgrade: first launch re-scores your library in a throttled background pass, so some tracks will switch to a better match over the following hours (a new "follow algorithm upgrades" toggle lets you freeze picks instead); the player setting is now multi-select (your old choice carries over); the notch's auto-hide switches are now separate from the desktop overlay's; and Intel Macs get in-app auto-update from this version on — but v1.5.0 itself needs one last manual install.

New

  • Select several players at once instead of one
  • YouTube Music and Spotify in a browser are properly supported as players — in whichever browser you pick; lyrics sync precisely to the page's own progress, with a one-click self-test
  • YouTube Music (LyricFind) and Kuwo join as lyrics sources — eight in total now
  • The UI is available in Traditional Chinese, as a third interface language alongside Simplified Chinese and English
  • Cantonese songs get word-aware Jyutping readings; all four romanization languages now default to on
  • Hand-picked lyrics can be locked against automatic rematching (new toggle, applied retroactively)
  • Background lyric upgrades can be turned off entirely — settled picks then stay put until you re-search yourself
  • A first-time lyrics search can be stopped — the song settles as "no lyrics" instead of searching forever
  • Six new global hotkeys, with conflict detection and on-screen feedback
  • The notch's expanded view is composable: track-info header, playback controls, next-line preview, lyric-offset control and per-row cover art are all toggleable
  • The notch's top row is configurable too: each ear picks its module (title / artist / album / cover / controls / elapsed / remaining / none), the equalizer can be turned off or moved to either ear (and was redrawn), lyrics can be hidden entirely, pause-collapse is optional, and the minimum width is much lower
  • The notch animates in and out — pausing shrinks the card back into the notch instead of it vanishing — and gained its own lyrics alignment setting
  • The overlay, menu bar and notch settings pages are all editors now, with live preview and reset-to-defaults; menu-bar lyrics gained an alignment mode; a one-time tip shows that ⌘-dragging moves the icon
  • Menu bar: a progress icon next to the lyrics (fills bottom-up with playback, Kugou-style), playback controls on hover, and adjustable lyric font weight and size
  • Desktop overlay: a hover control row (expand to lyrics window / settings / close), a ⚙ quick-settings menu with a standalone lyric-search mini window, an unlock button right on the overlay, and an alignment setting; the control capsule is smaller and uses a liquid-glass look (macOS 26+)
  • Desktop overlay: stroke color presets (white text + black stroke, black text + white stroke), with black text + white stroke + follow-cover as the new default look, and the settings preview now replicates the real word-by-word karaoke fill
  • Duet lyrics show a speaker indicator, so you can see who's singing
  • The lyrics window's right pane is now Play History, replacing the play queue: day-grouped records with paging — or your local pending listens when Last.fm isn't connected
  • The Lyrics Manager gained sorting (11 orderings), an offset column, album search, a placeholder row while a song is being searched, candidate covers in the decision panel, and round labels on multi-round searches
  • Settings shows lyrics-library stats, with translations split into source-provided vs machine-translated
  • The lyrics search shows each source's availability and failure reason, and Settings gained a one-click lyric-source test
  • The Dock icon has a right-click menu (Settings / Lyrics Manager / Lyrics Window / Last.fm), the lyrics window's title bar gained a settings button, and the "no lyrics" / "network failed" empty states offer a Search Lyrics button right there
  • The About page was redesigned — live GitHub star count, usage and copyright notes, and a third-party licenses list
  • Every outbound network request is audit-logged, and the diagnostics export got much richer
  • In-app auto-update now covers Intel Macs
  • The player picker is an icon grid; trusted players show their real icons

Fixed

  • Now-playing covers got a batch of accuracy fixes: artwork sent by the player itself is used first, wrong-edition album art is corrected, and Apple covers doubled in resolution
  • Searching lyric candidates dropped from minutes to seconds in the worst case
  • NetEase rate limiting is handled properly now — exponential backoff, a steadier endpoint, and no more false "rate limited" labels on sources that simply had no match
  • AMLL's embedded translations were never actually read; machine translation no longer skips repeated chorus lines
  • Translation or romanization lines no longer vanish over whitespace variants
  • The LRC [offset:] tag now works end to end, including the web page
  • The overlay's playback buttons register clicks where they're drawn
  • Notch lyrics stay readable on bright covers
  • Adopting a search result from the lyrics window no longer permanently freezes that song's lyrics
  • Rematch shows its actual outcome instead of a generic "no conclusion"
  • The Lyrics Manager highlight follows track changes; its filter bar no longer reflows on selection
  • The menu-bar panel opens over fullscreen apps and dismisses on Space change
  • Platform promo lines and credit/staff lines are filtered out of lyrics
  • Lyrics-window polish: background tinting no longer over-saturates near-black covers, and the volume capsule doesn't linger after playback stops
  • The Settings window keeps a fixed title; windows are listed in the Window menu; Dock reopen only auto-opens lyrics when nothing else is open
  • Importing an iCloud backup actually downloads it and validates it first
  • The "now scrobbling" count no longer goes stale, and "Nth listen" no longer shows a frozen old count for songs you haven't played in a while
