Supported players

Nine players, one set of lyrics.

Whichever player you listen in, the lyrics turn up in the same places: on the desktop, under the notch, in the menu bar. Turn on several players at once, or let Lyrimuse follow whichever one is playing.

  • Apple MusicLyrics for the radio too, and Apple's own lyrics
  • SpotifyThe version you're hearing, and Spotify Web too
  • YouTube MusicDesktop lyrics while you listen in the browser, and ads you can skip
  • Amazon MusicUses the lyrics it saved, and the next song is ready
  • KKBOXLyrics that keep up in Listen With
  • QQ MusicThe exact QQ song, and the next one ready
  • NetEase Cloud MusicNetEase's translations on screen, and lyrics ready on shuffle
  • Kugou MusicUses the lyrics Kugou already saved
  • Soda MusicSoda's own official lyrics, ready in the feed

Questions

Can I use several players?

Yes. Turn on as many as you like in Settings, or choose Auto-detect and Lyrimuse follows whichever player is playing.

Does a player that isn't listed work?

Often, yes. If it tells macOS what it's playing, you can add it in Settings. To ask for proper support, request it on GitHub.
