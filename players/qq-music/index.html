QQ Music lyrics, tucked under the notch.

With QQ Music in the background, the lyrics move along line by line in the capsule under the notch. Hover to see the next line and the progress bar.

Made for QQ Music

The exact song you're playing

For songs you've played or saved in QQ Music, Lyrimuse can tell exactly which QQ song it is, so a live take or a cover with the same title doesn't slip in.

The next song is ready

Lyrimuse looks ahead in QQ Music and prepares the lyrics and translations of the next few songs, so they're there the moment the song changes.

No sign-in

Lyrimuse doesn't need your QQ Music account. Pick QQ Music in the setup guide and you're set.

Everything else works the same in all nine players: desktop lyrics, the capsule under the notch, the menu bar, the Lyrics Window, translations, pronunciations and Last.fm scrobbling. Try it on the home page
