Compare

Lyrimuse, LyricsX and Lyric Fever, side by side.

Three open-source lyrics apps for the Mac, compared on what each project's own repository, README and release notes say it does.

Checked on 4 October 2026

Lyrimuse

GPL-3.0 · v1.9.0 · macOS 14+

Nine players, Chinese apps among them, and YouTube Music or Spotify in a browser. Twelve lyric sources scored together. Lyrics in four places on screen.

LyricsX

MPL-2.0 · v1.6.3 · macOS 10.11+

The long-standing Mac lyrics app, for Apple Music, Spotify and a few local players. Its last release came out in April 2022.

Lyric Fever

MIT · v3.3 · macOS 15+

Built around Spotify and Apple Music, with a fullscreen view and a karaoke popup. Its lyrics come mainly from Spotify.

How Lyrimuse picks lyrics

Every candidate, scored together.

For each track it asks all its sources, scores every candidate on the same scale and keeps the decision. Open it to see each source's score and why the winner won.

Is LyricsX still maintained?

Its last release is v1.6.3, published on 29 April 2022. The main branch was last changed in May 2022; since then only the branch that syncs community translations has moved. It still installs, and it's the only one of the three that runs on macOS 13 or earlier.

Which one fits you?

  • You mainly listen in Spotify or Apple Music, your Mac runs macOS 15 or later, and you like a fullscreen lyrics view: Lyric Fever fits.
  • You play music in QQ Music, NetEase Cloud Music, Kugou, Soda Music, KKBOX or Amazon Music, or listen to YouTube Music in a browser: Lyrimuse is the only one of the three that works with them.
  • Your Mac runs macOS 13 or earlier: LyricsX still installs there, though it hasn't had a release since 2022.
